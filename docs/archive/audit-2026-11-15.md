## How to read the numbers

Luma = mean over the screen minus HUD and viewmodel (0-255), `x` = RT / game; tint = sum |rgb/sum(rgb) - game rgb/sum| x 100
(`tools/lights/sheet.py`). One frame per mode, so a pose's second decimal is noise: lights that flicker, enemies and
animated geometry differ between the three captures (6-2's Gabriel, P-2's searchlights, 3-2's walls).

Verdict: **good** = relight within 0.8-1.25x and tint under 8; **minor** = 1.25-1.5x, or a tint of 8-15, or something visibly
different that the mean misses; **problem** = above 1.5x, tint over 15, or a visible defect. A ratio on a dark pose
(game luma under 12) is a small absolute difference but a big visible one (the RT frame shows rooms the game leaves black).

## Ranked problems (most visible first)

1. **8-2 night side (pose "8-2 b"): relight 1.79x, tint 15, day-lit facades where the game is a dark street.** The game
   frame is dark blue-black with one lamp's cone; relight shows both rows of facades in warm orange and pink, a pink sun
   wedge on the far buildings, and a lit pool. The diag run shows what it is not and what it is: the sky is not the cause
   (`_skyScale=0` changes nothing: 1.89x); the low side lights (the day's orange / blue-violet directionals 5 degrees above the
   horizon, unshadowed since the colour match) are the orange facade colour: switching them off takes the tint from 14.8 to
   6.6 and the ratio to 1.57x. The remaining 1.57x is not isolated (inferred, not tested): the pink main sun (35 degrees) still
   lights the far facades in the frame, and the day ambient is a candidate. All three are day-state lights that the game switches or culls per area (8-2's day/night is one level
   with a night backdrop behind `MirrorHider`); the RT scene applies them everywhere. 8-2 a / intro day / c are fine
   (0.92-0.96x), so this is gating by area or time of day, not the day colour. **Owner: lighting overrides / per-area light state
   (8-2 has no `data/lights` file).**
2. **7-3 forest: lit where the game is black.** Flashlight shadow 2.37x, flashlight 1.6x, tree b 1.69x (tint 19.5), tree room
   1.59x (tint 13.7). The fit moved the tree room from 1.96 to 1.54 (above); the rest is fill lights that 7-3's taste rule
   deliberately leaves alone (not isolated further). The red tree light is
   right, the white spots and fills are the extra light. Tint 19-20 on tree b is red walls reading orange-white. **Owner:
   lighting overrides (a lower bound than 0.5x on the 7-3 spots, fills in 7-3 and P-2), then exposure / colour match.**
3. **0-2, every pose, 1.4-1.7x** (v1 shadow a 1.72, decals 1.61, v1 shadow b 1.54, river 1.48 with tint 15.7, door 1.41,
   0-4 terminal 1.41). Colour is right (tint 1-3), brightness is the problem, so it is the lights, not the colour match.
   0-2 has no light file. **Owner: lighting overrides (classify and fit 0-2 and 0-4; same recipe as 7-3 / 0-1).**
4. **4-4 chair, 1.68x (pathtrace 1.70).** The hall's ceiling bays and walls are lit in RT, where the game's room is dim
   with a dark floor. No override file for 4-4 and the level is in checkpoint state. **Owner: lighting overrides.**
5. **6-2 Gabriel, 1.61x, tint 18 (pathtrace 24.5).** The game shows black walls; RT adds a red cast on them, and the colour
   match has little to work with (near-empty channels, as in docs/relight.md "Area colour"). **Owner: exposure / colour
   match** (then lights: 6-2 has no file).
6. **3-2 (eye 1.40, tint 11; wall edges 1.33, tint 10-13; moving walls 1.79-1.89).** Haze is small here (1.39 -> 1.34 with
   `vol.enabled=0`); the shipped overrides already take 1.86 -> 1.39. What is left is the eye's emission and bloom and the
   walls' lift on a nearly black pose (6.6 -> 11.8 luma). Tint 10-13 is RT staying more orange-yellow than the game's red.
   **Owner: lighting overrides / emissive; colour match.**
7. **8-2 portal side 1 and 2, 1.47-1.51x (tint 2).** Colour exact; the room's floor and the walls beside the doors are lifted.
   Same room as "building boil" (1.23x) but darker pose. **Owner: exposure (auto exposure target) / lighting.**
8. **Haze in dark red rooms: P-2 vent smoke (relight 1.28x, 0.88x without haze), 6-1 coffins (1.29 -> 1.12), 6-1 candles
   1.45, 0-2 door red metal's orange cloud.** In pathtrace P-2 vent smoke is 1.04x, so relight's haze and pathtrace's differ.
   **Owner: haze / volumetrics** (docs/volumetrics.md; max density, the shoulder and the particle clouds: the cloud

9. **P-2 v1 reflection: pathtrace 1.99x against relight 1.22x** (tint 17.8 relight / 9.4 pathtrace). A dark pose (game luma 7.6)
   with the ceiling lit in pathtrace only. P-2's two orange searchlights sweep on their own clock (docs/path-tracing.md,
   "P-2: the orange floor"), so a single frame can catch one; the difference in the tint (RT frames greyer than the game's
   deep red) is consistent in both. Needs a second capture (or a burst) before it counts. **Owner: noise / pathtrace

10. **8-2 lamp flicker and 8-2 c: lamp light shapes and wall colour.** Mean luma is right (1.07 / 0.92), but the room's walls
